1 — Direct scheme test (same-origin smoke test)

Navigates this window to a blob:/filesystem: page. Confirms the scheme works for top-nav and shows what the URL bar displays. No lookalike domain here (that's step 2) — this just proves the plumbing and lets you read the scheme's URL format. Press Back to return.

2 — Cross-origin lookalike test (the real bypass)

Host frame.html + common.js on a lookalike domain you own, put that origin below, and embed it in a sandboxed iframe with allow-top-navigation. The iframe builds a blob:/filesystem: URL inheriting the lookalike origin and redirects the top window to it. If the landed page appears with the lookalike domain in the URL bar and no warning, the bypass works.

What to observe
